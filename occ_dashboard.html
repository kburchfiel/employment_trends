Employment Trends Dashboard

Source: Current Population Survey data from IPUMS.
This graph shows the estimated number of Americans, for each occupation, whose EMPSTAT value was either "at work" or "has job, not at work last week".
Note: due to low sample sizes for many occupations, these results should be interpreted with caution. Estimates for shorter intervals are less reliable than those for longer ones.
